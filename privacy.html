Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 18, 2026

Overview

Transport Pull is designed to help users document transport pickups, drop-offs, and damage records.

Camera

Transport Pull uses camera access only when you choose to take pickup, drop-off, or damage photos.

Location

Transport Pull may use your location to record the city, state, latitude, and longitude associated with a transport record.

iCloud and CloudKit

Transport record history may be stored in the user's private iCloud account using Apple's CloudKit service.

Private CloudKit records are associated with the iCloud account signed into the user's device.

Files and Storage Providers

Users choose where exported photos and transport files are saved. These locations may include iCloud Drive, Dropbox, Google Drive, or other storage providers available through Apple's Files system.

Data Collection

Transport Pull does not operate its own advertising, analytics, or user-tracking service.

The developer does not sell personal information.
